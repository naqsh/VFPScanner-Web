## What counts as an e-invoice

An e-invoice is an invoice issued, sent and received in a **structured electronic format** that a computer can read without a person retyping it. Accepted formats:

- **XRechnung**, the German format based on the European standard EN 16931
- **ZUGFeRD from version 2.0.1**, a PDF with the structured data embedded
- Other formats that meet EN 16931

A PDF sent by email is **not** an e-invoice. Neither is a scanned paper invoice.

Not covered: invoices to consumers (B2C), small invoices up to €250, travel tickets, and certain tax-exempt supplies.

## What a FoxPro ERP has to change

Most FoxPro systems produce invoices with `REPORT FORM`, print them or turn them into PDFs. That output is made for people, not machines. To issue e-invoices, four things usually change:

1. **The data.** EN 16931 needs fields many older systems never stored in a clean way: VAT IDs for seller and buyer, a VAT category per line, standard unit codes, payment terms and bank details, and a buyer reference where the customer asks for one.
2. **The output.** Instead of (or next to) the printed report, the system must produce XRechnung XML or a ZUGFeRD PDF with embedded XML.
3. **Validation.** Each invoice should be checked against the official rules before it is sent, so a missing VAT ID is caught by you, not by your customer.
4. **Receiving.** Since 2025 you must be able to receive e-invoices too: store the XML, show it in readable form, and get the data into your purchasing process.

The hardest part is rarely the XML. It is finding where invoice data really lives, how clean it is, and which code builds it. In FoxPro applications that logic is often spread across forms, reports and macro-built commands.

## Your options

| Option | What it means | When it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Bridge** | A small program reads the invoice data from your FoxPro tables and produces XRechnung or ZUGFeRD. The FoxPro system stays as it is. | The deadline is close, and the rest of the application works. |
| **Migration** | Invoicing is rebuilt in .NET and SQL Server as part of moving the application. | A migration is planned anyway, and there is time before your date. |
| **Bridge, then migration** | The bridge keeps you compliant now; the migration follows on its own schedule. | Most FoxPro ERPs with a 2027 date. |
