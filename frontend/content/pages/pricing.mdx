## Notes

- Prices are shown without VAT. VAT is added where it applies, based on your country and VAT ID.
- The assessment price depends on the application size the scan measures, after removing duplicates and old copies. You know the size before you pay.
- If you go ahead with a migration, the assessment fee is credited against it.
- Migrations are quoted per phase after the assessment, so you can stop after any phase.
