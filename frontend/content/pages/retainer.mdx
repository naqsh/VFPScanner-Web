## What is included

- **Fixes** for problems found after go-live.
- **Small changes**: new fields, reports, rules and screens.
- **Updates**: .NET and library security patches applied and tested.
- **Format changes**: when e-invoicing formats or IRS file specifications change, the outputs are updated.
- **A named engineer** who knows your application, not a ticket queue.

## How it works

You agree a number of hours per month up front, in writing, and you can cancel monthly. Current prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing).
